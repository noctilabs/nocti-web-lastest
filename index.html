The production gap

AI doesn't fail on the model. It fails on the context.

Most AI never reaches production, and it's almost never the model. To run in your operation, AI has to know how it works: your permissions, your exceptions, the rules nobody wrote down. That lives in your team's heads, which is why the demo works and production doesn't.

The Fix

We extract how your operation runs

We extract how your operation actually runs, and turn it into the memory your agents run on: live, every day, on the stack you already use.
